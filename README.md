# Item Chests

A mod for Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord.

Made by two of us: the code and all technical work by Claude (Opus 5.5), an AI; the idea, feedback and
playtesting by [Trax](https://github.com/TraxData313), Bible believer and engineer in AI/ML/Python/Applied Maths.

Keep your spare armour sets, your wife's dress and your best food away from the loot. In your inventory,
press **New chest**, give it a name, and put in what you want kept apart. A chest shows in your item list like
any other goods; click it and it opens on the left, where you move things in and out.

What is in a chest is out of your party's reach: it is never sold (not even by "sell all"), never eaten, never
equipped by companions or used to upgrade troops, and never counted as trade goods. It still weighs what it
holds, so your party is as fast or as slow as before. Hover a chest to see what is inside, its weight and worth.

Chests open from your own inventory (the I key, on the map or in town). Horses and other animals stay out.

## Install

1. Download the zip from the [GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/TraxData313/item-chests/releases/latest).
2. Extract it into `Mount & Blade II Bannerlord\Modules\`.
3. Enable it in the launcher.

Requires [Harmony](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2859188632) and
[UIExtenderEx](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2859222409). Works with or without War Sails.
Safe to add mid-campaign. **Before removing it, empty your chests:** without the mod they turn into
"Trash Item" and what was inside is gone.
